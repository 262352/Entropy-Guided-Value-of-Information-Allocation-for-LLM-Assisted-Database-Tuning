A knob is one DBMS parameter. A group is a small set of knobs modified
together. A mode adds a proposal distribution and expert provenance to a
group. An action specifies an alternative search state; it is distinct
from a configuration submitted for execution. An expert is an LLM role;
an owning expert is one whose declared domain includes the knob. The
controller applies the deterministic feasibility, sampling, and decision
rules. Throughput/latency are measured performance metrics, utility is the
normalized log metric, and gain is a difference in utility. These terms
are used consistently throughout the method.

\subsection{Deterministic Action Construction}

Candidate groups are individual knobs and confirmed small interaction
groups. A group eligible for retirement contains one or two knobs, each meeting
the residence/coverage rules, with no protected knob or supported active
interaction broken. Active interaction partners are protected when their
conditional improvement probability is at least $0.7$ and expected gain
exceeds $\delta$. Domain representation and activation parents are retained.
A replacement admits the same number of new knobs as it retires, at most
two. Groups violating active capacity $d_a$ or sparse patch constraints are
excluded. The target on a review round is the eligible group with lowest
conditional gain; if none exists, the lowest-gain active group is a
region-only target. Non-review rounds also use a region-only target. With execution index
$t$, exchange review is due when $t>n_0$ and $(t-n_0)\bmod6=0$.
Coverage and re-entry are due when $(t-n_0+1)$ is divisible by 3 and 8,
respectively; both are disabled in the final $w_{\rm end}$ trials.

Each action slot has a deterministic candidate list. Reshape variants use
inner, current, and outer radii and bounded center suggestions. The exchange
slot considers inactive groups in descending conditional gain. For a
combined action, retained partners come from confirmed interactions with
the incoming group, ordered by joint conditional gain. Each variant gets
four legal preliminary probes with common random seeds; the largest
expected positive probe gain retains the slot. Equal scores favor fewer
changed coordinates, then sorted knob identifiers, centers, and widths.
The keep action is always present. No empty slot is filled by repeating
another action. After expert approval and edits, scores are recomputed.

Mode admission is deterministic within every state. Merge identical knob
groups and retain their contributing experts and alternative patches.
Sort modes by conditional gain. First select the highest-ranked mode
covering each not-yet-represented expert, in fixed role order while
capacity permits; then fill remaining mode slots by rank. This enforces
Equation~\ref{eq:ct-accounting} before any quota arithmetic. If no legal
mode exists, use the projected legal fallback distribution or terminate
with the execution checkpoint if even that is empty.

For state comparison, audit sizes are equalized across all admitted modes
and states; keep the same random seeds for unchanged distributions.
Configuration duplicates are one point with all sources retained.
Credit and quotas are computed on each state's frozen audit. This yields
$b(a)$ before $G_t(a;U)$ is evaluated. Thus a reshape that changes which
mode deserves candidate capacity can change the preferred structural
action, even when its active knobs stay the same.

An exchange must pass the paired mean/probability criteria against both
keep and the strongest admissible reshape of its outgoing group. If no
reshape is approved or feasible, keep supplies the comparison. A combined
action must satisfy the same tests. Posterior draws use the joint
covariance on all compared points, not independent marginal samples.
The 128-point cap includes anchor, preliminary and radius probes, state audits,
reserved-source audits, and information shortlist points. Shared points
are reused. If needed, drop combined first, then exchange; retain the
incumbent reshape when testing an exchange. Remove low-ranked optional
radius probes if still necessary, recomputing summaries on the retained
set. Preserve equal audit counts and the keep reference throughout.

\subsection{Regions, Executed Evidence, and Gate Records}

Region notation suppresses a mode index: each $c_j,r_j$ belongs to the
mode being compared. Numerical coordinates use declared global linear/log
normalization. Inner/outer probes vary legal directions in the affected
sparse group. Joint posterior best configurations determine radius
quantiles, so correlated coordinates share samples. A recommendation is
clipped both relatively and absolutely; edited centers are part of the
re-scored state, not applied after the comparison. Shrinking requires
executed points with $(z_j(x)-c_j)/r_j<-0.25$ and $>0.25$. Discrete
preferences use legal actual alternatives and have no numerical radius.

Distinct executed values differ by at least $0.005$ in the controller's
normalized coordinate. Two are required except for Boolean knobs (one),
categories ($\min(2,|\mathrm{choices}|-1)$), and integer domains with fewer
available alternatives. Numerical changes below $\min(0.01,0.1r_j)$ do not
establish local coverage. Only successful sparse local executions supply
that coverage; full-domain references do not. A joint trial can supply
coverage to its changed knobs without identifying separate causal effects.
Context resets clear local windows and fresh coverage while preserving
lifetime history.

Each adaptive prediction is recorded before execution. Its residual
uses latent variance plus learned observation noise; failed executions
produce no residual. Coverage is the fraction of the last at most twenty
successful adaptive predictions inside the nominal $95\%$ interval.
Initial IID measurements are training data, not in-sample calibration
residuals. The gate needs eight out-of-sample predictions and coverage
at least $0.7$. When it fails, qLogNEI and scheduled trials continue to
collect evidence without committing a posterior-driven state change.

Event records distinguish the number of region variants generated,
expert-approved variants, cases with enough improving draws, calibration passes,
paired-comparison passes, and adopted actions. Report both the gate pass
fraction among adaptive rounds and adoption fractions among eligible
region decisions. Expansion, contraction, center shift, and hold are
recorded per numerical region, alongside magnitudes and credible intervals.
Information records include gate eligibility, standard-error rejection,
selection, and scheduled override. These denominators separate a lack of
opportunities from a mechanism that proposes changes but fails its tests.

\subsection{Quota, Trial Selection, and Cost Details}

Candidate capacity satisfies $N_g\geq4|E|$ for the expert-facing floor.
Group quotas use largest-remainder rounding after integer floors; ties
follow canonical group order. Since $N_{\rm rem}\geq0$, they sum to $N_g$.
Let $g_e=v_e/\sum_{e'}v_{e'}$ for expert emphasis. Expert-facing shares are
\begin{equation}
 b^{\rm expert}_{e,t}=4+\operatorname{RoundSum}_e\!\left[
 (N_g-4|E|)\frac{0.02+g_e}{\sum_{e'}(0.02+g_{e'})}\right],\quad |E|=4.
\end{equation}
These shares enter the next expert request; current executable mode
quotas remain $b_h$. Audits are part of those quotas. Reference and
cross-domain sources do not receive group credit unless they also carry
a group source. The reserve uses up to twelve reference points, or six
on coverage rounds, up to four reviewed cross-domain patches, and the
scheduled coverage/re-entry points. Unused slots are not topped up.

The compiled bundle $\mathcal Q_t=\textsc{Compile}(A_t,R_t,\bar x_t,\mathcal P)$
contains the mode samplers, selected legal scopes, anchor, validated patch
seeds, and reviewed cross-domain patches. $\textsc{Fill}$ starts with frozen
audits and fills remaining mode quotas using these samplers. Patch seeds
are inserted only within their group's quota, then locally expanded;
ordinary sampling supplies the rest. It does not query experts again.
Patches incompatible with the selected action are omitted.

$\textsc{RecordState}$ stores the selected active set and regions, marks
newly admitted knobs with their entry round, and resets their residence
coverage counters without awarding an executed test. Initialization builds
the failed-configuration set from all initial execution records.

$\textsc{Reserve}$ receives the same bundle, memory $M_t$, the forbidden
set $\mathcal V_t$, and capacity $N_r$. Reference configurations are legal
full-space IID draws. Reviewed cross-domain configurations are executable
patches applied to the compiled anchor. Coverage proposals target active
knobs with the fewest successful local tests and insufficient distinct
values, using legal alternatives around their stored centers. Re-entry
proposals target the longest-waiting inactive legal knob/group and required
partners; ties use knob identifiers. Memory supplies active residence,
coverage counts/values, waiting times, region records, and attempted/failed
configuration keys. Coverage takes precedence over re-entry collisions.
The proposal carries its source and scheduled target through selection.

All samplers, patch insertion, and audits reject every configuration in
$\mathcal V_t=\{x:(x,y,s)\in L\}$, including all failed attempts, before
occupying a slot. Canonical-key deduplication combines group and reserved
pools and unions source labels; the final union is filtered against
$\mathcal V_t$ again. Frozen audit labels keep their audit-source sets; later pool deduplication
adds executable provenance without changing those labels. Duplicate slots
are not additional capacity, and rejection sampling obeys its fixed attempt limit. If the final pool is
empty, terminate at the checkpoint rather than rerun a failed configuration.

The selected state's frozen audits define the information label and
remain fixed under every fantasy. Ties in best audit utility use
configuration keys. Common inner latent normals are shared across
candidates and fantasies; the $F$ outer noisy fantasy draws are independent.
Equation~\ref{eq:ct-mc-error} estimates variability over these outer draws.
It does not estimate inner entropy bias or certify predictive calibration.
qLogNEI is scored in log space with cutoff $\max\log a+\log\eta$.
Shortlisting first retains each available source's best eligible point,
then fills by qLogNEI, to at most sixteen points.

\subsection{Expert and Surrogate Protocol}

The four experts use role prompts on \texttt{deepseek-flash}, temperature
$0.2$, disabled thinking, JSON output, and at most 6,000 output tokens.
All completed configurations, performance records, and failures enter the
run history. Fixed instructions, workload and domain descriptions, and append-only
compact history precede volatile scopes, anchors, and shares. Actual
provider cache usage, all retries, reviews, token counts, and latency are
recorded. Required schema errors permit at most three proposal attempts.
Experts approve legal action identifiers and action-tagged patches;
prospective scopes become executable only if the action is selected.
Cross-domain disagreement drops that patch. Empty/failed proposals retain
a scope-projected legal distribution, then fall back to the anchor-centered
sampler. They create no utility observations.

The exact GP uses output standardization, learned Gaussian noise, double
precision, and at most 75 fitting steps. Numerical coordinates follow
linear/log normalization, ordinal values normalized indices, categories
one-hot indicators, Boolean values two indicators, and every knob an
activation mask. Inactive values are zero with mask zero. Activity restricts
generation, not model input dimension. With $n$ successful observations and
encoded dimension $d$, a dense factorization costs $O(n^3+n^2d)$ with
$O(n^2+nd)$ storage. Posterior comparisons use the bounded reference set.
LLM usage, GP fitting, state-audit generation, posterior sampling, and
workload execution are distinct timing categories.

Feasibility handling clips/rounds values, resolves activation dependencies,
repairs declared forbidden/resource combinations, and validates the full
configuration. Repairs count toward patch size and movement. Invalid or
repeated candidates are rejected before workload execution. Failed trials
consume $T$ and remain in expert history and the attempted set, but do
not train the utility GP or increase successful coverage.
